Common Questions

Is the app free to use?

Yes! All current features of the app like the Health Meter, daily questions, and basic mood tracking are completely free.

Does it work for long-distance couples?

Absolutely. In fact, 40% of our users are in long-distance relationships. Features like the "Secret Notes" and real-time mood updates are perfect for staying connected from afar.

Is my data private?

Privacy is our top priority. Your chat and journal entries are securely stored and encrypted in transit. We do not share your personal conversations with any third parties.
